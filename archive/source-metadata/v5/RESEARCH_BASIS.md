# Wave 5 — Research & Product Rationale

Wave 5 is built around a shift from **reactive job search** to **proactive opportunity access**.

## Why these modules exist

1. LinkedIn's recruiter products increasingly use AI and natural-language intent to interpret qualifications and adjacent skills. This supports role-adjacency mapping, a machine-readable public profile and a coherent evidence graph.
2. Executive-search assessment is broader than keyword matching and considers track record, market/functional knowledge, leadership, motivation and fit. This supports relationship management, evidence depth and executive-introduction assets.
3. LinkedIn/Edelman thought-leadership research shows that useful, perspective-shifting insight can influence visible and hidden decision-makers. This supports the distribution engine, visibility radar and authority campaign.
4. PwC's 2026 Middle East research shows strong AI adoption and continued emphasis on data readiness, governance, cloud, resilience and capability-led growth. This supports the narrative radar and GCC-oriented opportunity thesis.
5. Google Search Central continues to emphasize people-first, original, first-hand content. This supports a deliberate bias toward fewer original ideas distributed well rather than automated high-volume content.

See `executive-growth/research/sources.json` for exact URLs and source-specific findings.
