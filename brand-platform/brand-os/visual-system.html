Brand Identity

Refresh the identity without redesigning everything

A compatibility-first visual system: preserve what already works on the main site and Digital Card while giving internal products a consistent executive design grammar.

Typography

Executive clarity first

UI / body: Inter, Segoe UI, Arial. Display: Georgia / Times as a restrained editorial accent. The main website keeps Josefin Sans; the Digital Card keeps its current type choices.

Composition

Dark executive shell + light evidence surfaces

Internal products use navy as the environmental color, cyan for technology/action, gold for impact, and white/paper surfaces where evidence or research needs maximum readability.

Do not homogenize everything

Brand consistency is created by shared tokens and language, not by forcing the homepage, Digital Card, CVs and every internal tool to become visually identical.

Main websiteKeep current Josefin Sans, section structure, project/gallery behavior and heritage blue/yellow language.
Digital CardKeep current luxury navy/cyan/gold visual and its current layout.
Internal platformsAdopt Brand v3 tokens, reusable components and signature language.
Research / PerspectivesUse calmer editorial layouts and light reading surfaces where long-form clarity matters.
