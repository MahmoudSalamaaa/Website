Maturity Model

From fragmented to adaptive

A five-level executive model for discussing the progression from isolated technology assets to governed, measurable and adaptive institutional capability.

Level 1

Fragmented

Siloed systems, local ownership, inconsistent data and reactive operations.

Level 2

Managed

Basic standards, repeatable delivery, clearer ownership and emerging controls.

Level 3

Integrated

Connected platforms, shared data, interoperable processes and coordinated delivery.

Level 4

Governed

Portfolio governance, architecture controls, measurable service ownership and decision transparency.

Level 5

Adaptive

Continuous optimization, evidence-led investment, resilient platforms and rapid institutional learning.

This is a proprietary working model for executive discussion, not a substitute for a formally validated external maturity standard.
