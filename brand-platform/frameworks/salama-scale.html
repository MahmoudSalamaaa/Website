Signature Framework

SALAMA SCALE

A five-dimension working framework for evaluating whether transformation is strategically aligned, institutionally capable, architecturally coherent, well led and executable.

Strategy

Business alignment, technology direction, priorities and measurable outcomes.

Capability

People, process, skills, operating model, ownership and institutional readiness.

Architecture

Applications, integration, data, infrastructure and technical guardrails.

Leadership

Governance, stakeholders, vendors, change, accountability and decisions.

Execution

Delivery, adoption, operations, measurement and continuous improvement.

How to use it: assess a transformation across all five dimensions, identify the weakest link, then convert the gap into ownership, milestones and evidence.
