How I think about technology

Seven concise principles designed to make the leadership philosophy clear and reusable across articles, interviews, presentations and project discussions.

Technology creates value when it becomes institutional capability — governed, adopted and measurable.
01 · Capability over softwareA system going live is a milestone, not the outcome.
02 · Architecture reduces complexityArchitecture should make ownership and change easier, not produce documentation for its own sake.
03 · Evidence over assumptionsDecisions should be traceable to data, operating reality and explicit trade-offs.
04 · Platforms over silosShared capability and interoperability matter when institutions must work together.
05 · Ownership over handoffsTransformation needs clear decision rights from strategy through operations.
06 · Adoption is part of architectureIf people cannot reliably use and operate the capability, the technical design is incomplete.
07 · Outcomes over activityPrograms should be judged by institutional performance, not the volume of meetings or releases.
