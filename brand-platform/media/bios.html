Extended bio

Mahmoud Salama is a technology and digital transformation executive and enterprise architect with 18+ years of experience across software delivery, enterprise applications, systems integration, data, technology governance and large-scale digital transformation. His career has developed from hands-on solution building and software delivery into government technology, enterprise architecture and executive technology leadership. He has worked across Egypt and the GCC, including more than four years living and working in Muscat and embedded delivery experience within the Oman Ministry of Education environment. In Egypt, his work has included architecture, governance, integration and data initiatives supporting complex public-sector ecosystems. His portfolio includes national-scale procurement and medical-supply technology, enterprise data environments, Microsoft Dynamics 365 integration, software/application delivery and institutional adoption. Mahmoud’s working philosophy is that digital transformation should be treated as institutional capability rather than software implementation: strategy, architecture, platforms, data, governance, adoption and outcomes must reinforce one another. He writes and speaks about digital government, enterprise architecture, technology leadership, data and transformation at scale.
