Content Operating Model

Publish less. Compound more.

A repeatable editorial system for turning evidence-backed technology leadership ideas into long-form perspectives, executive briefs, carousels and reusable diagrams.

One idea → multiple executive assets

The purpose is not content volume. It is to turn one evidence-backed point of view into formats suitable for different audiences.

01

Perspective

Long-form article anchored in evidence and a clear point of view.

02

Executive Brief

Two-page decision summary: problem, framework, implications and actions.

03

LinkedIn Carousel

6–10 slides translating the idea into a visual narrative.

04

Quote / Diagram

One memorable line or model that reinforces the signature language.

Publishing rule: every asset must sit under one content pillar, connect to a real problem, and link back to a canonical page or framework.
