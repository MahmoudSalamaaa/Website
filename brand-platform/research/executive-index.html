Benchmark Concept

Methodology before marketing

A benchmark concept designed to become a defensible research product only after source coverage, scoring rules and review standards are mature.

Design principle: the index stays in “methodology mode” until enough comparable public evidence exists. This avoids turning personal branding into unsupported ranking claims.
