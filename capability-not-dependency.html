DEEP DIVE · OPERATING MODEL

Build Capability, Not Vendor Dependency

External partners can accelerate delivery, but the organization should retain enough capability to understand, govern and evolve its critical technology.

Ownership is more than contract ownership

An organization needs internal understanding of architecture, data, integration, security, operations and product priorities even when implementation is outsourced.

Design knowledge transfer into delivery

Documentation, paired work, architecture decisions, operational runbooks and internal participation should be deliverables, not optional activities near contract closure.

Keep strategic boundaries visible

Vendor components should integrate through explicit contracts and ownership boundaries. This reduces the risk that a supplier becomes the only party able to change the system safely.

Measure dependency, not just delivery

A project can be on time while increasing long-term dependency. Governance should ask whether internal teams can operate, diagnose, procure and evolve the capability after handover.

Use partners where they add leverage

The objective is not to avoid vendors. It is to combine specialist external capability with durable institutional knowledge and decision authority.
