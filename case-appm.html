CASE STUDY · CONTINENTAL PROCUREMENT

Africa CDC APPM — African Pooled Procurement Mechanism

An end-to-end in-house digital platform designed to support pooled-procurement operations across all 55 African Union Member States.

Challenge

Translate a multi-country pooled-procurement mechanism into a governed digital platform with clear roles, workflows, data and operational handoffs.

Context & scale

The platform was designed for continental scope across all 55 African Union Member States; this is design scope, not a claim of active use by all 55.

Role

Led the internal Digital Transformation team responsible for the platform’s end-to-end design and delivery.

Decision

Build the capability in-house to retain architectural knowledge, workflow ownership and the ability to evolve the platform.

Architecture

Portal and manufacturer prequalification, frontend/backend, data, workflows, integrations, notifications and deployment.

Delivery

Implemented as a live production platform with documented operational flows and role boundaries.

Outcome

Production digital platform supporting the pooled-procurement operating model.

Evidence

QA evidence covers 30 workflows and 13 role IDs.

Lessons

For multi-country platforms, role clarity and workflow governance are as important as the user interface; scope language must distinguish design intent from adoption.
