CASE STUDY · NATIONAL HEALTHCARE

MedIQ — National Healthcare Procurement & Medical Supply Ecosystem

National-scale platform transformation combining executive technology ownership with architecture, integration, production transition and live-service governance.

Challenge

Modernize a national procurement and medical-supply ecosystem while preserving operational continuity across a large institutional network.

Context & scale

38K+ users, 5,900+ government healthcare entities, ~90K affiliated operational locations and 2,000+ suppliers.

Role

Executive ownership through UPA’s 54+ person technology organization, connecting architecture, delivery, migration, integration, readiness and production accountability.

Decision

Re-architect and rebuild the core with strong internal ownership while keeping external ecosystem components and partner-delivered services explicitly attributed.

Architecture

Procurement and supply workflows, data/reporting, shared controls and enterprise integrations including SAP, Dynamics 365, GS1/GLN and healthcare APIs.

Delivery

Migration, reconciliation, testing, performance/security validation, training, cutover, stabilization and transition into live operations.

Outcome

Verified outcomes include 80% manual workload/process reduction, 60% procurement-cycle reduction and 70% reporting-time reduction.

Evidence

Conservative annualized operating evidence includes 1.2M+ delivery permits, 35K+ purchase orders and 125K+ approved procurement requests; 39K+ training supported.

Lessons

National transformation continues after go-live: operational ownership, evidence, adoption and integration discipline are part of the architecture.
