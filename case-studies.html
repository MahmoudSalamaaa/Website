MedIQ / Unified Procurement & Medical Supply Ecosystem

A governed 10-platform ecosystem supporting procurement, approvals, inventory, distribution, supplier processes, and supply-chain controls across 12,000+ public-sector entities.

Portfolio scale + approximate measured improvements

Context & challenge

Fragmented workflows, multi-agency approvals, limited visibility, and national-scale governance requirements.

Leadership approach

  • Defined architecture across ten connected platforms.
  • Established workflow, RBAC, auditability, integration, quality, and release-governance standards.
  • Aligned executives, operations, engineering teams, vendors, and public-sector stakeholders.

Evidence-based outcomes

  • Up to 90% less manual handling in selected workflows.
  • Approximately 40% reduction in end-to-end procurement-cycle time.
  • Up to 74% reduction in specific workflow durations.

Technology

ASP.NET, C#, React.js, Node.js, GraphQL, MySQL

Led architecture

Egypt's National Health Sector Data Warehouse

A governed enterprise data environment for 100+ government institutions, processing 12M+ records daily and supporting executive analytics.

Verified operational scale + approximate reporting improvement

Context & challenge

Disconnected operational data, inconsistent master data, labor-intensive reporting, and limited cross-institution visibility.

Leadership approach

  • Designed pipeline, operational-data-store, master-data, and validation architecture.
  • Established ownership, quality, lineage, and executive-reporting controls.
  • Connected Power BI and Apache Superset to governed analytical layers.

Evidence-based outcomes

  • 12M+ records processed daily.
  • Approximately 70% faster executive-report preparation.
  • Improved cross-institution visibility and data consistency.

Technology

PostgreSQL, SQL, Data Pipelines, MDM, Power BI, Apache Superset
