Assets, GS1 & Geographic Mapping

Connecting standardized medical-asset identity to field location, mapping, maintenance and executive visibility.

GS1 GTIN/GLNMapTilerQR/GPSMedical assets

Context

National asset visibility is not only an inventory problem. The architecture needed standardized identifiers, field-operable location capture, geographic mapping and integration with the wider MedIQ ecosystem.

Architecture decision

I designed the geographic asset-mapping architecture using MapTiler and the QR/GPS-oriented location approach. GS1 APIs were integrated to retrieve and apply GTIN and GLN identifiers for standardized medical-asset identification and geographic asset-location mapping.

Delivery model

The application spans mobile and web experiences. Team implementation was executed under my management; my personal ownership is the geographic mapping architecture, MapTiler selection, QR/GPS approach and technical direction.

Evidence context

The asset-management evidence base covers 242,816 medical devices across 164 entities, with EGP 431M request value, 2,860 repaired devices, 504 repair requests and 381 maintenance requests in the cited operational period.

Why this architecture matters

Identifiers and coordinates become useful only when they serve operational workflows. The design joins standardized identification, location, maintenance and executive visibility rather than treating mapping as a decorative layer.

Personal architecture decision

MapTiler + QR/GPS

Geographic mapping architecture and technology selection are personally owned.

Standards evidence

GS1 integration

GTIN and GLN are the confirmed identifier scope; no broader GS1 standards are claimed without evidence.
