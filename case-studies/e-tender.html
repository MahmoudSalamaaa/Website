Workflow governance

MedIQ E-Tender

Turning a complex procurement process into a governed enterprise workflow rather than a digital replica of paper forms.

Enterprise workflowRBACSegregation of dutiesAuditability

Context

Public procurement tendering is a multi-stage governance process, not a simple form workflow. The platform needed to represent business stages, approvals, committee responsibilities, auditability, sensitive decisions and downstream award outputs.

Scope

MedIQ E-Tender spans 10 major steps, 30 operational stages, 15 Chairman approvals and 20+ roles/permission groups. The workflow covers items/specifications, estimated value, preparation and approval, publication, envelopes, administrative evaluation, technical evaluation, grievances, financial evaluation and award.

Architecture principles

  • RBAC and segregation of duties across committees and controlled actions.
  • Workflow gates that prevent invalid transitions.
  • Auditability and traceability for approvals and decisions.
  • Sensitive approval controls including OTP where applicable.
  • Controlled conversion of award outputs into Sales Items, Price Lists and Framework Agreements.

My role

I originated the current electronic tendering/practice idea and led end-to-end requirements, process/workflow shaping, architecture, data/integration governance, development oversight, testing, deployment, operation and improvement.

Why it matters

Complex workflow architecture is where business process, permissions, data state and accountability converge. The success criterion is not “digitized forms”; it is a controlled digital operating model that preserves governance while reducing manual fragmentation.

Architecture focus

RBAC · SoD · workflow gates · traceability · controlled approvals · downstream integration.
