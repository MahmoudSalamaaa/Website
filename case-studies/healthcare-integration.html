Cross-Government Healthcare Integration

A case in contract discipline, role clarity and production-status accuracy across independent government and healthcare systems.

REST APIsData contractsHealthcareGovernance

Context

Cross-government healthcare integration requires more than connecting endpoints. Each external organization has its own data contracts, ownership boundaries, authentication model, operational dependencies and change cadence.

Role clarity

My ownership covers architecture, technical/integration design, data-flow decisions, governance and team leadership for ECIC, MoH/HMIS, GAHAC and Blood Bank integrations. Application/API implementation for ECIC and GAHAC was performed by team members under my leadership. I do not represent that team implementation as personal coding.

Production status

Blood Bank systemsProduction confirmedArchitecture, integration design, data flows and execution management.
MoH / HMISIntegration and architecture confirmed; production status not promoted hereArchitecture, design, decisions and implementation management.

Integration pattern

The practical model includes REST APIs, contracts/schemas, mapping/transformation, authentication, bidirectional flows where needed, background sync/retry, reconciliation, versioning and backward-compatibility considerations. The emphasis is on reliable contracts and operating ownership rather than forcing every system into one technology label.

Separate hands-on API evidence

Separately from those team-delivered integrations, I personally designed and developed production healthcare REST APIs for secure beneficiary, prescription and dispensing-data exchange with external health entities, including token-based authentication, structured JSON contracts, GTIN-enabled product identification and standardized error handling.

FHIR position

I do not claim production FHIR implementation. The transferable experience is enterprise healthcare interoperability: contracts, resources/data models, security, versioning, mapping, traceability and operational governance.

Confirmed production

ECIC + Blood Bank

Production status is explicitly confirmed for these integrations.

Do not overclaim

GAHAC and HMIS integration/architecture are confirmed, but this case does not label them production without stronger confirmation.

Hands-on coding

Personal coding claim is reserved for the separate production healthcare REST API, not generalized across all integrations.
