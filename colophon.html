COLOPHON

A website should show how its thinking is built.

This site is designed as an executive evidence and knowledge platform—not only a portfolio or job-search landing page.

01

Content model

Career evidence, case studies, architecture, projects, field notes, operating principles and current interests are separated so visitors can choose their own depth.

02

Evidence discipline

Material claims stay within documented boundaries. Internally authored evidence is not presented as independent validation.

03

Progressive enhancement

Role-aware navigation, command search, installable metadata and subtle transitions enhance the site without making core content depend on them.

04

Change philosophy

A living Now page and Field Notes make ongoing thinking visible without rewriting the professional record every time an idea changes.

Built as a durable public knowledge surface, not a campaign page.
