PUBLIC DECISION NOTE

Operational Data & Analytics: Separate Transactional Work from Analytical Read Paths

A decision note on ODS/data-warehouse boundaries, reporting performance and governed analytical access.

Context

Operational platforms need reliable transactions while leaders and analysts need cross-domain reporting and historical views.

Decision

Keep transactional workloads authoritative for operations and provide governed analytical/read paths through ODS, warehouse or reporting layers as appropriate.

Why

Separating workloads reduces pressure on production transactions and creates clearer data-quality, transformation and access boundaries.

Trade-offs

Analytical copies introduce latency, reconciliation and lineage responsibilities. Near-real-time is a design choice, not a default requirement.

Governance

Define ownership, freshness, lineage, access and reconciliation explicitly so the analytical layer does not become an undocumented second source of truth.

Public boundary

This note explains a reusable decision pattern. It intentionally excludes confidential topology, credentials, security configuration, internal endpoints and other operationally sensitive details.
