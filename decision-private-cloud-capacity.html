PUBLIC DECISION NOTE

Private-Cloud Capacity: Optimize for Service, Not Idle Headroom

A FinOps-aligned decision note on private-cloud capacity, cost and resource optimization without pretending private infrastructure behaves like public cloud.

Context

Private-cloud environments have finite purchased capacity and different cost signals from consumption-priced public cloud.

Decision

Treat capacity, utilization, service criticality and growth together. Optimize resource allocation while preserving justified resilience and recovery headroom.

Why

Raw utilization alone can drive unsafe consolidation, while permanent over-provisioning hides cost and capacity pressure.

Trade-offs

The right headroom varies by workload, recovery objective and procurement lead time. A single utilization target is not an operating model.

Governance

Review demand, committed capacity, forecast growth and service risk together. This is FinOps-aligned resource governance, not a claim of formal FinOps implementation.

Public boundary

This note explains a reusable decision pattern. It intentionally excludes confidential topology, credentials, security configuration, internal endpoints and other operationally sensitive details.
