PUBLIC DECISION NOTE

RAG Grounding & Governance: Approved Sources Before Fluent Answers

A public decision note for grounded enterprise AI, retrieval, source awareness and human judgment.

Context

Enterprise assistants can produce fluent answers even when organizational evidence is incomplete or inaccessible.

Decision

Ground answers in approved retrievable sources, preserve source awareness, respect permissions and make unsupported questions explicit.

Why

The objective is not merely better prose. It is a more inspectable relationship between an answer and the information the organization trusts.

Trade-offs

Retrieval quality depends on source quality, chunking, metadata, freshness and evaluation. Citation reduces ambiguity but does not guarantee correctness.

Human boundary

Consequential decisions retain human accountability. The assistant should expose evidence and uncertainty rather than manufacture confidence.

Public boundary

This note explains a reusable decision pattern. It intentionally excludes confidential topology, credentials, security configuration, internal endpoints and other operationally sensitive details.
