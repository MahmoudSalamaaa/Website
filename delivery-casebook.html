Reporting and controlled NL-to-SQL

Context and need

Make operational information accessible across complex procurement and healthcare workflows.

Personal responsibility

Designed reusable reporting patterns and selected Telerik Reporting. Directed data readiness and a controlled DeepSeek query capability.

Analysis, engineering and delivery

Parameterized reports, standardized outputs and SQL validation support governed access. NL-to-SQL scope is read-only SELECT, allowlisted and subject to RBAC. Broad production AI rollout is not implied.

Outcome and scale

Recorded evidence supports 20+ production reports; reporting and AI deployment statuses are distinguished.

Delivery lesson

Model access must remain inside the same data and permission boundaries as other applications.
