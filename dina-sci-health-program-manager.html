Final-day revision · 10-minute checklist

Before the written assessment

  1. Start with priorities: safety, health needs, vulnerable groups and service continuity.
  2. Structure the answer: assess → plan → implement → monitor → correct → report.
  3. Know the distinctions: DIP tracks activities; IPTT tracks indicator performance; outputs are not outcomes.
  4. Remember calculations: achievement = actual ÷ target × 100; utilisation = spent ÷ budget × 100; variance in percentage points = actual % − planned %.
  5. For safeguarding: protect, listen, report through authorised channels; do not investigate yourself.
  6. For donors: reconcile figures, verify eligibility, document approvals and report limitations transparently.
  7. For internal SCI tools: PRIME, PART and PMM are expanded in the original vacancy; learn their names but do not claim hands-on experience without evidence.
  8. For leadership examples: use verified personal examples from MSF and clinical nutrition, stating your exact role and outcomes.
