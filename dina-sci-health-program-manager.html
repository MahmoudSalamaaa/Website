07 · Safeguarding decision map

A study reminder, not a substitute for SCI's approved reporting protocol.

Immediate safety→Listen, don't investigate→Report promptly→Specialist response

Protect confidentiality. Never promise absolute secrecy or confront the alleged perpetrator. Urgent care or protection referrals follow authorised procedures.

Recall: can you investigate yourself?No. Record essential facts and refer through designated safeguarding channels.
