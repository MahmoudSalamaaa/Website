Choose the CV by the job — not by the company.

There are five current CV versions: one broad Executive CV and four specialist versions, all refreshed with the latest verified transformation, architecture, delivery and engineering evidence. Each is built for a different recruiter search pattern and ATS keyword cluster. Use the closest version to the vacancy scope; do not create a new permanent CV for every application.

Technology & Digital Transformation Executive

Positioning: Strategy | Enterprise Architecture | Platforms | Programs | IT Operations.

This is the balanced version. It shows executive technology leadership without forcing the profile too far toward architecture, software engineering, program management, or operations.

Best for: roles where the employer wants one senior leader to connect strategy, architecture, digital transformation, enterprise platforms, delivery, operations, vendors, security/resilience and team leadership.

Typical titles:

  • Head of Technology / Technology Director
  • Director of Technology & Digital Transformation
  • Digital Transformation Director / Head of Digital Transformation
  • CTO in organizations where the remit is broad enterprise technology, not purely product engineering
  • IT & Digital Transformation Director
  • Enterprise Platforms / Technology Transformation Director
  • Senior technology leadership roles with mixed architecture + delivery + operations scope

Choose it when the JD repeatedly mentions:

  • Technology strategy and roadmap
  • Digital transformation
  • Enterprise platforms
  • Architecture and modernization
  • Programs / portfolio delivery
  • IT operations and resilience
  • Budget, vendors, governance and executive stakeholders

Do not make it first choice when: the vacancy is clearly a pure Enterprise Architect, Software Engineering Manager, PM/Program Manager, or classic IT Operations role. In those cases the specialist CV gives stronger ATS concentration.

  • Integration Architecture Lead / Director
  • Platform Modernization / Architecture Transformation Lead

  • Enterprise Applications / Platforms Architecture Director
  • Digital Transformation Architect / Digital Government Architect

Strong JD signals: target-state, current-state assessment, architecture principles, roadmaps, solution design, integration/API strategy, production healthcare interoperability, data governance, NFRs, security-by-design, resilience, build-vs-buy, vendor architecture, TCO, technical debt, modernization.

Not the first choice for: pure cloud-specialist roles, dedicated security/CISO roles, or FHIR-specialist roles unless the vacancy is broader and those are only components.
