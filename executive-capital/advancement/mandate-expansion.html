Mandate Expansion Map

The strongest promotion case is a shift from managing activities to owning an enterprise capability.

CapabilityManager-level framingExecutive-level framing
Technology strategyDepartment prioritiesEnterprise technology portfolio aligned to institutional outcomes
ArchitectureProject-level designEnterprise architecture standards, integration principles and decision governance
DataReporting deliveryEnterprise data governance, quality, master data and AI-ready foundations
DeliveryProject executionPortfolio governance, prioritization, benefits and vendor accountability
OperationsSupport and availabilityService ownership, continuity, resilience and measurable experience
PeopleTeam managementCapability building, succession, leadership bench and operating model
AIAd-hoc toolsResponsible AI portfolio, use-case governance, data readiness and adoption
Use this in promotion conversations: “The opportunity is to consolidate these responsibilities into one accountable enterprise mandate, with clear metrics and governance, rather than continue treating them as separate technology activities.”
