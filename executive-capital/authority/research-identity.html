Research Identity & Contribution Registry

Your research and analysis work should become a persistent professional asset, not disappear inside project folders. ORCID provides a durable identifier for research, scholarship and innovation contributions.

Recommended sequence: create ORCID → add current affiliation and research outputs → connect future papers, datasets and reviews → add ORCID to the personal site author/entity page and academic CV. ORCID is free and persistent, but it is not an identity-verification system.
