Public Evidence Policy

Brand strength is reduced, not improved, by claims that cannot be defended.

Personal metricsPublish only metrics already supported by the evidence bank or a directly verified source. Never round upward for marketing effect.
Job titlesUse the documented title or an explicitly labeled functional descriptor. Do not inflate title hierarchy.
Case studiesSeparate personal contribution from team / institutional outcomes. State mandate, approach and result without implying sole ownership.
External researchKeep source links and dates. Distinguish sourced fact from Mahmoud’s interpretation.
ConfidentialityNever publish restricted procurement details, credentials, non-public commercial terms, personal data, security-sensitive architecture or confidential employer material.
AI-generated contentAI can assist synthesis, drafting and formatting. Final claims, opinions and professional representations remain human-reviewed and evidence-bound.
