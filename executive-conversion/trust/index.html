Evidence-first claims

No metric, role, certification or client should be strengthened unless it exists in an approved source or evidence bank.

Confidentiality

Public artifacts use approved, non-confidential summaries. Internal work samples should be redacted before sharing.

AI use

AI may structure, match or rewrite existing evidence; it must not create missing experience or endorsements.

Decision support

Scores and probability tools are planning heuristics, never representations of a recruiter’s actual decision.
