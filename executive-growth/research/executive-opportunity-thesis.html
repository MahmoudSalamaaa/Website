Executive opportunity thesis · 2026

From visible candidate to discoverable executive capability

The strongest career strategy for a senior technology leader is no longer a sequence of applications. It is a portfolio of discoverable evidence, trusted relationships, original market insight and timely access to organizations when their mandate is changing.

1 · Search is becoming semanticRecruiter systems increasingly interpret intent and adjacent skills. Career data therefore needs a coherent capability graph rather than keyword stuffing.
2 · Executive mandates emerge from changeLeadership changes, transformation programs, AI/data/cloud investment, M&A and risk pressure can create demand before an advertised vacancy exists.
3 · Reputation reduces cold-start frictionUseful research, speaking, case studies and third-party references give advocates and search consultants reasons to remember and forward the profile.
4 · Evidence needs different depths30-second proof, 2-minute relevance and 10-minute evidence should coexist instead of forcing every stakeholder into one portfolio journey.
5 · The system must measure conversionViews are only useful when they lead to replies, introductions, interviews, advisory requests, speaking opportunities or higher-value responsibilities.
