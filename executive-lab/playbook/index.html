Technology Leadership Playbook

How I structure technology leadership.

A concise operating philosophy derived from large-scale delivery and governance work. It is intentionally principle-led and evidence-linked rather than a list of buzzwords.

Chapter 1

Strategy & Portfolio

Translate strategy into a governed portfolio with explicit outcomes, ownership, risk and benefits.

Chapter 2

Architecture

Use principles and decision records to reduce accidental complexity and clarify ownership.

Chapter 3

Data

Create trusted decision foundations through ownership, master data, quality and governed analytics.

Chapter 4

Delivery

Make dependencies, risks, decisions, acceptance criteria and production readiness visible.

Chapter 5

Vendors & Partners

Govern outcomes, interfaces, responsibilities, evidence and transition — not activity alone.

Chapter 6

Change & Adoption

Treat training, communication, support and ownership as part of the product, not post-go-live extras.

Chapter 7

Operations & Resilience

Design service ownership, monitoring, incident response, continuity and knowledge transfer early.

Chapter 8

Executive Reporting

Report outcomes, blockers, risk, decisions and value — not only activity and technical detail.

Operating principle: technology value is realized when architecture, delivery, data, people and operations converge around measurable institutional outcomes.
