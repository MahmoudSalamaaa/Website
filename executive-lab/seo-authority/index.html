Search Authority / Knowledge Graph Kit

Make the professional identity machine-readable.

A consistency checklist for Google, LinkedIn, GitHub and the website. The goal is not keyword stuffing; it is unambiguous entity, role and evidence alignment.

Identity consistency

Use the same canonical name, headline, location, profile image, LinkedIn/GitHub links and core positioning across public surfaces.

Structured data

Use Person/ProfilePage on primary profile surfaces, Article on thought leadership, and BreadcrumbList on deeper public sections. Validate before deployment.

Evidence architecture

Link role pages to case studies and case studies to the executive portfolio so claims have a discoverable evidence path.

Index discipline

Index public proof/content surfaces. Keep private career tooling, trackers and interview generators noindex and out of the sitemap.

Content clusters

Build coherent clusters around digital government, enterprise architecture, technology leadership, data governance and GCC delivery.

Measurement

Track Search Console queries, page impressions, recruiter-view visits and CTA clicks. Improve pages based on evidence, not vanity traffic.
