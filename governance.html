EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

A recognition page should increase trust, not inflate claims.

The rule is simple: identify who was recognized, state my contribution separately, and make the strength of the source visible. That keeps the portfolio celebratory without becoming promotional fiction.

01 · ATTRIBUTE

Name the recognized entity.

Institution, platform, programme or project comes first. Personal attribution is never implied from proximity.

02 · CONTRIBUTE

State my role separately.

Leadership, architecture, delivery or engineering contribution is described on its own terms and within evidence boundaries.

03 · VERIFY

Expose the evidence strength.

Official source, public recognition, programme context or privately retained evidence are not treated as equivalent.
