Grounded Enterprise AI: RAG, Governance and Human Judgment

Enterprise AI becomes more useful when answers are grounded in approved information, traceable to sources and bounded by human judgment.

Grounding changes the problem

Retrieval-augmented generation shifts the task from asking a model to remember the organization toward giving it relevant, governed context for each request.

Source quality matters more than volume

A larger corpus is not automatically better. Approved sources, clear ownership, freshness and useful metadata improve the chance that retrieval supplies the right context.

Governance belongs in the architecture

Access control, data classification, logging, evaluation and escalation should be designed with the AI workflow. They are not policy text to add after a prototype succeeds.

Citations support judgment

Source-aware responses let users inspect the basis of an answer. Citation does not make an answer automatically correct, but it creates a better path for verification and challenge.

Keep humans at consequential boundaries

High-impact decisions need explicit human accountability. AI can retrieve, summarize and surface patterns while people retain responsibility for decisions that carry material operational consequences.
