How I lead architecture

Pragmatic decisions, not architecture theatre.

Real enterprise environments impose regulatory, budget, vendor, timing and operational constraints. The goal is not the most fashionable architecture; it is the strongest defensible decision that can be delivered and operated.

Leadership & decision modelArchitecture evidence
Clarify the business outcome, constraints, risk and regulatory requirements.
Compare options across architecture, dependencies, lifecycle cost and operational impact.
Use a PoC when uncertainty materially affects the decision.
Lead implementation, testing, security/performance validation and production readiness.
Measure operating evidence and feed it back into the roadmap.
