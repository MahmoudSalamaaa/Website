TYPOGRAPHY EXPERIMENT 02

Twenty body fonts. One title style.

The title treatment stays in the same visual direction you already like. Only the ordinary text changes, so you can judge which font gives the best balance of executive presence, readability and personality.

How to compare Read the paragraph first. Then look at the small UI labels and metrics. The best choice should work well in both.
