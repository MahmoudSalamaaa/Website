THE EXECUTIVE PROBLEM

Modernize without losing operational control.

MedIQ sits across procurement, supply, inventory, assets, budgeting, reporting and integrations. The transformation therefore had to be treated as an operating-system change rather than a conventional software release: re-architecture and rebuild, data migration and reconciliation, testing, security and performance validation, training, parallel operation, cutover, stabilization and production transition all had to move as one governed programme.
ARCHITECTURE

Clearer application, data and integration boundaries

Shape a platform model that can evolve while institutional workflows remain governed and usable.

DELIVERY GOVERNANCE

One accountable transformation path

Connect roadmap decisions, engineering execution, testing, readiness, adoption and production ownership.

OPERATIONS

Technology ownership continues after go-live

Monitoring, incidents, support, onboarding, adoption and continuous improvement remain inside the operating model.

OPERATING CHAIN

One platform across the full healthcare supply workflow.

The platform connects institutional demand to procurement and award, supplier operations, delivery and receipt, inventory and assets, budgeting, data and reporting — with shared controls and enterprise integrations across the chain.
