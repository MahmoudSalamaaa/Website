Final review — one page of decisions

Use this after the bank, not instead of it. The goal is to keep your opening messages, response frameworks and panel questions mentally available without cramming.

10 messages the panel should remember about you

Enterprise systems leader, not only infrastructure.
Healthcare + public-sector operational complexity transfers well to MSF.
Field continuity and user reality come before elegant architecture.
You know the WaCA NetSuite / UniField / SIRA transition evidence and its uncertainty boundaries.
You can govern M365, Entra, Azure, MDM, backup/DR and integrations.
You treat access, data quality and reconciliation as business controls.
You develop teams with clarity, feedback, recognition and follow-through.
You understand safeguarding, confidentiality, conflicts and vendor integrity.
You will verify before overclaiming any WaCA architecture.
You are transparent about French and actively improving it.

Six frameworks to answer almost anything

Incident

Impact → contain → stabilize → communicate → recover → verify → RCA/improve.

Architecture

Business need → constraints → options/trade-offs → control → operability → exit/DR.

Project

Outcome → stakeholders → scope → risks/dependencies → delivery → adoption → evidence.

People

I ensure / involve / develop → expectation → SBI facts → listen → feedforward/support → follow-up → HR if formal issue.

Vendor

Criticality → due diligence → access/data → SLA/security → COI/integrity → monitoring → exit.

Executive risk

Who/what is affected → consequence → current control → residual risk → options → decision needed.

Core facts/terms to be able to explain naturally

NetSuite HQ accountingUniField field finance/supplySIRA → UniField transitionSITS shared servicesHomere HR/payroll (movement evidence)DHIS2 ≠ EMRMDM + Conditional AccessRestore testing ≠ backup successSBI feedback modelI ensure · I involve · I developNeed-to-know confidentiality

Do not say

  • “WaCA definitely uses X” when your evidence is MSF-wide or OCB-only.
  • “I would investigate a safeguarding complaint myself.”
  • “I reset access” before validating authorization/role.
  • “Backups are green, so DR is tested.”
  • “The vendor needs permanent Global Admin.”
  • “My French is professional” if it is not.

Ask the panel

  1. What are the top three system risks/priorities for the next 12 months?
  2. What is the current NetSuite/UniField/SIRA target state and rollout stage?
  3. Which services are owned by WaCA versus SITS?
  4. What systems create the most incidents or user frustration today?
  5. How is the Systems team structured across HQ and missions?
  6. What would excellent performance after six months look like?
Day-before rule: stop trying to learn new niche facts. Rehearse your top 20, first-90-days answer, three real stories, safeguarding/confidentiality judgement, systems map and French opening. Sleep matters more than a 141st question.
