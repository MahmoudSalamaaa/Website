Manager operating model

  • Need-to-know handling of confidential operational, financial, personal and medical information.

4. Current technical anchors

Microsoft

Identity, endpoint and cloud

  • Entra emergency access: at least two cloud-only accounts, phishing-resistant authentication, monitored and tested at least every 90 days.
  • Intune: compliance + Conditional Access as a device trust signal.
  • Azure Well-Architected: Reliability, Security, Cost Optimization, Operational Excellence, Performance Efficiency.
NIST

Incident and risk model

  • CSF 2.0: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
  • SP 800-61 Rev.3 (2025) integrates incident response into broader cyber-risk management.
  • Use the framework to structure action; do not recite it without an operational response.
AWS

Govern, secure, recover

  • Control Tower/Organizations for a governed multi-account landing zone.
  • S3 Block Public Access + IAM/KMS/logging; Macie can help find sensitive S3 data.
  • Cross-account/cross-Region backup where justified and real restore testing.
Oracle NetSuite

WaCA ERP interview depth

  • Role-based permissions; keep Administrator membership tightly limited.
  • REST + OAuth 2.0 is Oracle's preferred direction for new integrations.
  • SDF supports version control, validation and structured deployment of customizations.

5. How to answer — panel-level rules

01

Lead with outcome

Medical/field continuity, finance integrity, supply traceability, confidentiality or service availability.

02

Name risk

Connectivity, sensitive data, privilege, duplicate transactions, unsupported lifecycle or vendor dependence.

03

Controls + owners

Who owns business decisions, who implements technical controls, and how exceptions are governed.

04

Prove it

Reconciliation, restore test, audit log, KPI, UAT sign-off, access review or incident evidence.

6. Facts to verify in the interview

  • Current production split between NetSuite, UniField and SIRA, and target-state dates.
  • Which NetSuite modules are live/planned beyond general accounting and P2P.
  • WaCA's exact SITS service boundaries and identity model.
  • Actual AWS workloads/ownership: written-test relevance does not prove the production architecture.
  • Current MDM product, ITSM platform, monitoring/backup stack and enterprise application inventory maturity.
  • Exact HR/HIS platforms owned or supported by this Systems team versus functional teams.
