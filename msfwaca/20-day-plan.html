Final ReviewStudy Coach

Daily protocol - 75 to 110 minutes

10 min

Cold recall

Answer yesterday's hardest questions before opening notes.

35 min

Learn / compare

One focused topic. Explain why each service/control is chosen.

25 min

Speak aloud

3-5 timed interview answers. Record at least one.

15 min

Repair

Rate Again/Hard/Good/Easy and schedule the next review.

Why this works

Retrieval practice

Testing yourself improves later retention more than repeated study. The system now reveals answers only after you attempt them.

Spacing

Reviews are distributed over time. Good/Easy answers return later; weak answers return sooner.

Interleaving

Mocks mix systems, AWS, people and scenarios so you must identify the right strategy rather than memorize blocks.

Self-explanation

For architecture choices, explain “why this” and “why not the tempting alternative.”
