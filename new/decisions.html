Decisions

Architecture is not a diagram. It is a sequence of choices.

My role is to turn ambiguity, constraints and competing priorities into a technical direction that can actually be delivered and operated.

01

Replace, not decorate.

For MediQ, the direction was a full re-architecture and replacement rather than a cosmetic upgrade.

02

Test before scale.

Design → test → measure → tune or scale → production.

03

Build for operations.

Background processing, retry, state management, monitoring, backup and DR belong inside the architecture.

04

Integrate deliberately.

Define boundaries, data flows, ownership, security and production readiness before connecting systems.

05

Choose for purpose.

Redis for caching, MapTiler for geographic asset mapping, Telerik Reporting for centralized reporting.

06

Make trade-offs visible.

Constraints, risk, cost, scalability, maintainability and operational impact are part of the decision.

Decision pattern

From problem to production.

01 · Frame
Business problem + constraints

Clarify the actual institutional problem, stakeholders, dependencies and non-negotiables.

02 · Compare
Alternatives + trade-offs

Evaluate architecture and technology options, including PoCs where needed.

03 · Decide
Architecture + standards

Set technical direction, ownership, security, data and integration patterns.

04 · Prove
Testing + readiness

Validate performance, security, migration, reconciliation and operational readiness.

05 · Operate
Production + continuity

Cut over, stabilize, monitor, learn and improve.

Good systems begin with clear decisions.
