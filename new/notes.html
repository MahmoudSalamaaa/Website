Notes

Short thoughts about systems, leadership and technology.

Not a corporate blog. A place for ideas worth keeping.

Architecture

Complexity belongs somewhere.

If users are carrying the complexity, the architecture probably did not.

Leadership

Technical leadership is decision ownership.

Consensus helps. Accountability decides.

Operations

Production is where architecture becomes real.

A diagram can be elegant while the system underneath is impossible to operate.

Transformation

Replacing a system is often easier than replacing assumptions.

The difficult part is not only code. It is process, data, habits, dependencies and trust.

Data

Data without governance creates noise.

Value appears when ownership, definitions, quality and decisions are connected.

Strategy

Technology strategy is a sequence of bets.

Make those bets explicit, affordable, reversible where possible, and aligned with institutional purpose.

Write to clarify.
Build to prove.
