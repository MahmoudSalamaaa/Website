FIELD NOTES

Ideas from the space between architecture and operations.

Short-form thinking: patterns, questions and lessons worth keeping. Not polished thought leadership; a public working notebook.

Architecture

The diagram is not the architecture

The real architecture lives in decisions: who owns a boundary, what happens when a dependency fails, how data moves, and how the system changes safely.

Transformation

Digitizing a bad workflow preserves the bad workflow

Transformation starts by questioning the operating model. Automation comes after the process has earned the right to be automated.

Leadership

Executive dashboards should expose decisions

A useful dashboard does more than report status. It makes exceptions, constraints, trends and decision points visible early enough to act.

AI

Grounding is a product feature

For enterprise AI, citations, source boundaries, evaluation and permission-aware retrieval are part of the user experience—not backend details.

Delivery

Go-live is the middle of the story

Adoption, support, stabilization, observability and feedback determine whether a launch becomes an operating capability.

Governance

Good governance reduces ambiguity

The strongest controls clarify who can decide, what evidence is required, how risk is surfaced and when escalation is justified.
