03 · FLAGSHIP NATIONAL PLATFORM

MedIQ — connecting procurement, supply and operations as one governed national ecosystem.

The challenge was not another application. It was creating a coherent technology environment across procurement, pharmacy, inventory, assets, integrations, reporting and support — while serving a national healthcare network with different users, institutions and operating needs.

01 · CHALLENGE

Fragmented institutional workflows

Procurement and medical-supply operations needed shared rules, traceable workflows, controlled access and a consistent operational view across multiple capabilities.

02 · LEADERSHIP

Technology ownership across the lifecycle

Executive accountability spans technology strategy, solution direction, delivery governance, integration/data governance, production operations and adoption.

03 · ARCHITECTURE

One ecosystem, governed by common controls

Shared workflow rules, RBAC and data-level permissions, document/audit controls, notifications, APIs and reporting connect demand, procurement, supply and operational services.

04 · OUTCOME

A national operating platform

MedIQ supports 38K+ users, 5,900+ government healthcare entities, nearly 90K affiliated operational locations and 2K+ suppliers across connected procurement and medical-supply capabilities. Verified outcomes include 80% less manual workload, 60% shorter procurement cycles, 70% faster reporting, 78% automation across targeted workflows, >90% sprint completion across tracked delivery cycles and 39K+ users/staff trained cumulatively.

04 · CONTINENTAL DIGITAL PROCUREMENT

Africa CDC APPM — digital infrastructure for pooled procurement across Africa.

UPA's internal Digital Transformation team designed and delivered the digital platform supporting the Africa CDC African Pooled Procurement Mechanism (APPM). My role was to lead the team and govern the platform from solution direction through production delivery — without conflating platform delivery with ownership of the APPM policy mechanism itself.

SCOPE

Continental procurement platform

Live portal and manufacturer-prequalification capabilities supported by frontend/backend services, a governed data layer, integrations and notifications.

DELIVERY

30 workflows · 13 role IDs

The implementation evidence covers 30 documented workflows and 13 role IDs, providing a controlled operating model for platform processes and permissions.

ARCHITECTURE

Workflow, data and integration as one system

Platform design connected business workflows, access roles, data services, integration points and operational notifications rather than treating them as isolated modules.

DESIGN REACH

55 African Union Member States

The platform was designed for continental use across all 55 AU Member States. This describes design scope and does not imply that all 55 are active users.
