Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be processed when you visit Mahmoud Salama's professional website, use its contact form, or follow links to third-party services.

This is a personal professional website and executive portfolio. It is designed to present career information, projects, professional writing and contact options. It is not designed for behavioral advertising or the sale of personal information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to pages and services served under mahmoud-salama.vercel.app, including the main website, project archive and Executive Portfolio where those pages link to this policy.

Third-party websites opened through external links are governed by their own privacy practices.

2. Information that may be processed

Information you choose to provide

If you use the contact form, the site may process the information you enter, including:

  • first and last name;
  • email address;
  • phone number, when provided;
  • message subject and message content; and
  • the page URL from which the message was submitted.

Technical information

Hosting, security and analytics services may process technical information associated with a visit, such as IP address, browser/device information, referrer, requested pages, timestamps, performance information and similar diagnostic data.

3. Contact form

The contact form is used to receive and respond to professional enquiries. Submitted fields are validated and sanitized before delivery. The server-side contact endpoint also applies request-size limits, origin checks, anti-spam controls and rate limiting.

An IP address may be used temporarily in server memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Contact messages are delivered through EmailJS to the configured recipient mailbox. After delivery, the message may be retained by the email provider according to normal mailbox-retention practices.

4. Analytics and performance information

When the main website is served on Vercel, it may load Vercel analytics/insights tooling to understand aggregate site usage and performance. This information is used to maintain and improve the website rather than to build advertising profiles.

Hosting infrastructure may also create standard request, security and operational logs needed to serve and protect the site.

5. Third-party services

The website may rely on service providers for hosting, contact delivery, security and linked professional profiles.

Vercel

Website hosting, delivery, operational infrastructure and site insights where enabled.

EmailJS

Delivery of messages submitted through the website contact form.

Cloudflare Turnstile

Human verification and abuse-prevention checks when Turnstile is enabled.

The website also links to services such as LinkedIn and GitHub. Opening those links transfers you to the relevant third party.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The website may use browser storage for interface preferences or experience state on some sections. Third-party hosting, analytics or security services may also use cookies or comparable technologies when necessary to provide their functions.

You can control or clear browser cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Restricting them may affect optional interface or verification features.

7. Retention and security

Personal information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, such as responding to a professional enquiry, maintaining correspondence, preventing abuse or meeting legitimate operational requirements.

Reasonable technical controls are used to reduce risk, including input validation, request limits, origin checks, anti-spam measures and HTTPS delivery. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so confidential credentials, sensitive personal records or restricted information should not be submitted through the public contact form.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact Mahmoud Salama to ask about personal information you submitted through this website, or to request correction or deletion where reasonably applicable. Depending on your jurisdiction, additional privacy rights may apply.

You can also contact directly by email instead of using the website form and may use browser privacy controls to limit cookies or local storage.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its contact workflow or service providers change. The effective date shown above identifies the current published version.
