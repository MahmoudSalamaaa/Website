Q01Healthcare

What is Revenue Cycle Management, or R C M, in the context of a healthcare technology platform?

Interview Answer

Revenue Cycle Management is the financial and administrative lifecycle around patient care: registration and eligibility, clinical documentation and coding, pre-authorization, billing, claim validation and submission, denial handling, payment, and reconciliation. The goal is accurate, compliant reimbursement with minimum manual friction and revenue leakage.

What is the current NPHIES technical exchange model?

Interview Answer

The current public Financial Services Implementation Guide uses H L 7 FHIR R 4 point 0 point 1. Q12Healthcare

What is the difference between eligibility, prior authorization, and claim adjudication?

Interview Answer

Eligibility asks whether the patient has applicable insurance coverage and benefits. Prior authorization asks whether the payer approves a planned service before it is delivered. Claim adjudication occurs after a claim is submitted and determines the payer's financial response, such as approval, partial approval, rejection, or further information requirements.

شرح مبسط

Q14Healthcare

Why can both prior authorization and a financial claim use the FHIR Claim resource?

Interview Answer

FHIR Claim supports different adjudication purposes. In the NPHIES authorization profile, Claim.use is fixed to preauthorization; for a reimbursement claim, the purpose is claim. The corresponding adjudication response is based on ClaimResponse. So the business meaning depends on the profile and use, not simply on the resource name.

شرح مبسط

لأن FHIR Claim تدعم أغراض adjudication مختلفة. في NPHIES يكون Claim.use في طلب الموافقة هو preauthorization، بينما في المطالبة المالية يكون الغرض claim. والرد يعتمد على ClaimResponse. لذلك نفهم المعنى من الـprofile والـuse، وليس من اسم الـresource وحده.

Q17AI

Explain ICD-10-AM, SBS, and AR-DRG without mixing them up.

Interview Answer

ICD-10-AM classifies diagnoses. SBS, the Saudi Billing System, standardizes the classification and billing of healthcare services and interventions in Saudi Arabia and is derived from ACHI with Saudi modifications. AR-DRG is a casemix classification that groups mainly admitted-care episodes into clinically similar groups expected to require similar healthcare resources. The systems interact, but they serve different purposes.

شرح مبسط

ICD-10-AM يصنف التشخيصات. SBS هو نظام الفوترة السعودي لتصنيف وفوترة الخدمات والتدخلات الصحية، ومبني على ACHI مع تعديلات سعودية. AR-DRG هو نظام casemix يجمع أساسًا حالات المرضى المنومين في مجموعات متشابهة سريريًا ومتقاربة في الموارد المتوقعة للعلاج. الأنظمة مترابطة، لكن لكل منها وظيفة مختلفة.

Q19Architecture

What is the architectural difference between denial prevention and denial management?

Interview Answer

Denial prevention is upstream: detect issues such as missing documentation, coding errors, payer-rule conflicts, eligibility problems, missing authorization, or medical-necessity issues before claim submission. Denial management is downstream: analyze rejected or partially adjudicated claims, identify the cause, provide missing information or corrections when appropriate, and process the next required action. A mature RCM architecture should feed recurring denial patterns back into upstream validation.

شرح مبسط

Denial Prevention يعمل قبل الإرسال: اكتشاف نقص التوثيق، أخطاء الترميز، تعارض قواعد الـpayer، مشاكل eligibility، نقص الـauthorization أو medical necessity. أما Denial Management فيعمل بعد الرفض أو الـpartial adjudication: تحليل السبب، توفير المعلومات أو التصحيح المطلوب، ثم تنفيذ الخطوة التالية المناسبة. والنظام الناضج يعيد أسباب الرفض المتكررة إلى قواعد الـvalidation لمنع تكرارها.

Q21Architecture

What makes a system truly microservices-based rather than simply a monolith split into many small applications?

Interview Answer

Microservices are organized around clear business capabilities and bounded contexts. Each service should be loosely coupled, independently deployable, expose a well-defined contract, and own its domain data and schema. Persistent shared schemas, chatty dependencies, or coordinated deployments reduce service autonomy and can create a distributed monolith. During migration, temporary shared-data arrangements may sometimes be necessary, but they should be treated as transitional compromises.

شرح مبسط

الـMicroservices تُبنى حول business capabilities وbounded contexts واضحة. كل service يجب أن تكون loosely coupled، قابلة للنشر بشكل مستقل، لها contract واضح وتمتلك domain data والـschema الخاصة بها. الاعتماد المستمر على shared schemas أو chatty calls أو deployments جماعية يقلل الاستقلالية وقد ينتج distributed monolith. أثناء الـmigration قد توجد حلول shared مؤقتة، لكنها تعتبر transitional compromise.

Q22Architecture

How would you migrate a legacy monolith to microservices without creating unacceptable business risk?

Interview Answer

I would normally prefer incremental modernization over a big-bang rewrite. I would identify a clear business capability, intercept and route its traffic where possible, build the replacement service, run the legacy and new implementations in parallel, validate the new path, and then retire the old capability. This is the Strangler Fig approach. If calls cannot be intercepted cleanly or the target component sits deep inside the monolith with upstream dependencies, I would use another incremental decomposition pattern rather than force Strangler Fig.

شرح مبسط

أفضل عادة modernization تدريجيًا بدل big-bang rewrite. أحدد business capability واضحة، وأعترض أو أعيد توجيه الـtraffic عند الإمكان، وأبني service بديلة، ثم أشغل القديم والجديد معًا وأتحقق من المسار الجديد قبل إيقاف الجزء القديم. هذا هو Strangler Fig. لكن إذا لم يكن من الممكن intercept للـcalls بسهولة أو كان الجزء المطلوب عميقًا داخل الـmonolith وله upstream dependencies، أستخدم pattern تدريجي آخر بدل إجبار Strangler Fig.

Q23Architecture

When would you use synchronous APIs versus asynchronous event-driven communication?

Interview Answer

I use synchronous communication when the caller needs an immediate response and the workflow naturally follows request-response semantics. I use asynchronous messaging when producers and consumers should be decoupled, when multiple consumers react independently, or when buffering and independent scaling are valuable. The trade-off is that asynchronous designs introduce challenges such as eventual consistency, ordering, error recovery, and distributed observability.

شرح مبسط

أستخدم synchronous communication عندما يحتاج الـcaller إلى رد مباشر ويكون الـworkflow طبيعيًا بنمط request-response. وأستخدم asynchronous messaging عندما أحتاج فصل producers عن consumers، أو تفاعل عدة consumers بشكل مستقل، أو buffering وindependent scaling. لكن الـasync يضيف تحديات مثل eventual consistency وordering وerror recovery وdistributed observability.

Q24AI

How would you explain the difference between Kafka and RabbitMQ without oversimplifying it?

Interview Answer

Kafka is fundamentally an event-streaming platform: it publishes and subscribes to event streams, stores them durably for configured retention, and supports replay and stream processing. RabbitMQ is a messaging broker that supports work queues, publish/subscribe, routing, topics, request/reply, and also streams. As a starting heuristic, Kafka is attractive when durable event history, replay, and high-scale stream processing are central; RabbitMQ is attractive when message routing, work distribution, and messaging patterns are central. I would still choose from actual delivery, ordering, retention, throughput, routing, and operational requirements rather than the product label alone.

شرح مبسط

Kafka أساسًا event-streaming platform؛ تنشر وتستهلك event streams، وتحتفظ بها لمدة محددة بحيث يمكن replay ومعالجة الـstreams. RabbitMQ هو messaging broker يدعم work queues وpublish/subscribe وrouting وtopics وrequest/reply، ويدعم streams أيضًا. مبدئيًا Kafka قوية عندما يكون event history وreplay وstream processing أساسيًا، بينما RabbitMQ قوية عندما تكون message routing وwork distribution وأنماط messaging هي الأساس. لكن القرار النهائي يعتمد على المتطلبات وليس اسم التقنية.

Q25Architecture

What is the role of an API Gateway in a microservices architecture?

Interview Answer

An API Gateway provides a controlled entry point between clients and backend services. It can perform routing and centralize cross-cutting concerns such as authentication, TLS or mTLS, rate limiting, logging, and request aggregation. I would keep domain business logic inside the services rather than concentrating it in the gateway, so the gateway does not become a tightly coupled central application layer.

شرح مبسط

الـAPI Gateway يوفر entry point منظمًا بين الـclients والـbackend services، ويمكنه تنفيذ routing وتجميع وظائف مشتركة مثل authentication وTLS أو mTLS وrate limiting وlogging وrequest aggregation. وأفضل إبقاء domain business logic داخل الخدمات نفسها بدل تركيزها في الـGateway حتى لا يصبح طبقة مركزية شديدة الـcoupling.

Q26Healthcare

What is idempotency, and why is it critical in healthcare claims and payment integrations?

Interview Answer

An operation is idempotent when multiple identical requests have the same intended effect on system state as one request. This is critical when retries are possible. If a claim or payment-related request times out, the caller might not know whether it was processed. An idempotent design allows a safe retry without unintentionally creating the business transaction twice.

شرح مبسط

العملية تكون idempotent عندما يؤدي تكرار نفس الطلب إلى نفس الـintended effect على حالة النظام كما لو تم إرساله مرة واحدة. أهميتها تظهر مع الـretries؛ فإذا حدث timeout أثناء claim أو payment transaction قد لا يعرف الـcaller هل تمت المعالجة أم لا. التصميم idempotent يسمح بإعادة الطلب بأمان دون إنشاء نفس الـbusiness transaction مرتين بالخطأ.

Q27Architecture

What is the difference between Retry and Circuit Breaker?

Interview Answer

Retry handles failures that are expected to be temporary, ideally with exponential backoff and a bounded number of attempts. Circuit Breaker is used when failures or latency persist; it temporarily stops calls to the unhealthy dependency so the caller fails fast instead of creating cascading load. Retries should also be used only with operations that are safe to repeat.

شرح مبسط

الـRetry مناسب للأخطاء المؤقتة، ويفضل باستخدام exponential backoff وعدد محاولات محدود. أما Circuit Breaker فيستخدم عندما تستمر failures أو الـlatency، فيوقف الاستدعاءات مؤقتًا للخدمة المتأثرة حتى لا تتحول المشكلة إلى cascading failure. والـretries يجب أن تكون على عمليات يمكن تكرارها بأمان.

Q28Architecture

What is the difference between Docker and Kubernetes?

Interview Answer

Docker provides tooling to build container images and run containers. A container is an isolated process packaged with the files and dependencies it needs. Q29Architecture

What are readiness, liveness, and startup probes in Kubernetes?

Interview Answer

A startup probe determines whether the application has successfully started. A readiness probe determines whether the Pod should receive traffic. A liveness probe determines whether Kubernetes should restart a container that is no longer healthy. Keeping these signals separate prevents a temporarily unready service from being unnecessarily restarted and helps avoid sending traffic to a service that cannot handle it.

شرح مبسط

Startup Probe يتحقق أن التطبيق بدأ بنجاح. Readiness Probe يحدد هل الـPod جاهز لاستقبال traffic. Liveness Probe يحدد هل الـcontainer أصبح غير سليم ويحتاج restart. الفصل بينهم يمنع إرسال traffic لخدمة غير جاهزة ويمنع restart غير ضروري لخدمة ما زالت تعمل لكنها غير جاهزة مؤقتًا.

Q35Security

What is the difference between RBAC and least privilege?

Interview Answer

RBAC assigns permissions to defined roles and then assigns users to those roles, instead of managing permissions individually for every user. Least privilege is the broader security principle that each user, service, or process should receive only the access needed to perform its function. RBAC is one mechanism that helps implement least privilege, but poor role design can still create excessive access.

شرح مبسط

في RBAC نربط permissions بالـroles ثم نربط المستخدمين بهذه الـroles بدل إعطاء كل مستخدم permissions منفردة. أما least privilege فهو المبدأ الأوسع: كل user أو service أو process يحصل فقط على الصلاحيات التي يحتاجها. RBAC وسيلة لتطبيق least privilege، لكن role سيئة التصميم قد تظل تعطي صلاحيات زائدة.

RTO, Recovery Time Objective, is the maximum acceptable time between service interruption and restoration. What are non-functional requirements, and why should they be defined before architecture selection?

Interview Answer

Functional requirements describe what capabilities the system must provide. Non-functional requirements describe required quality attributes and operating characteristics, such as availability, latency, scalability, security, privacy, recoverability, auditability, interoperability, and observability. Architecture must also account for constraints such as regulation, budget, and technology boundaries. These requirements and constraints shape architecture choices because two designs can deliver the same functionality while behaving very differently under scale, failure, security, or operational pressure.

شرح مبسط

الـFunctional Requirements تحدد القدرات التي يجب أن يقدمها النظام. أما الـNFRs فتحدد quality attributes وطريقة عمل النظام، مثل availability وlatency وscalability وsecurity وprivacy وrecoverability وauditability وinteroperability وobservability. كذلك يجب مراعاة constraints مثل regulation والbudget والtechnology boundaries. هذه المتطلبات والقيود تؤثر مباشرة على اختيار الـarchitecture لأن تصميمين قد ينفذان نفس الوظيفة لكن يختلفان تمامًا عند scale أو failure أو security أو التشغيل.

Q55Leadership

How would you run an architecture review without turning architecture governance into bureaucracy?

Interview Answer

I would make governance proportional to risk and reversibility. High-impact, hard-to-reverse, security-sensitive, regulatory, or cross-platform decisions deserve deeper review. Routine and easily reversible implementation decisions should remain with delivery teams inside agreed guardrails. Reviews should focus on requirements, trade-offs, dependencies, security, operability, cost, and migration impact—not personal technology preferences. Important decisions should be recorded, and reusable decisions should become reference patterns or standards.

شرح مبسط

أجعل مستوى الحوكمة متناسبًا مع المخاطر ومدى سهولة عكس القرار. القرارات عالية التأثير أو الصعبة في الرجوع عنها أو المتعلقة بالأمن والتنظيم أو عدة platforms تحتاج مراجعة أعمق. أما implementation decisions اليومية والقابلة للعكس بسهولة فتظل مع delivery teams داخل guardrails متفق عليها. والمراجعة تركز على requirements وtrade-offs والdependencies والsecurity والoperability والcost والmigration impact، وليس تفضيلات تقنية شخصية.

Q56Leadership

How would you measure engineering velocity without encouraging teams to game the metrics?

Interview Answer

I would not reduce engineering velocity or developer productivity to a single activity metric such as story points, lines of code, or commit counts. For software delivery performance, I would use the current five DORA metrics: change lead time, deployment frequency, failed-deployment recovery time, change fail rate, and deployment rework rate. I would use them primarily to track the same application or service over time, together with product outcomes, quality, reliability, and team-health signals. I would not use them to rank individual developers or blindly compare teams operating in different contexts.

شرح مبسط

لا أختزل engineering velocity أو developer productivity في activity metric واحدة مثل story points أو lines of code أو commit counts. بالنسبة للـsoftware delivery performance أستخدم DORA الحالية: change lead time، deployment frequency، failed-deployment recovery time، change fail rate، deployment rework rate. والأفضل استخدامها لمتابعة نفس application أو service عبر الزمن، بجانب product outcomes والجودة والreliability وteam-health signals، وليس لترتيب الأفراد أو مقارنة فرق تعمل في contexts مختلفة بشكل أعمى.

Q57AI

Explain SLI, SLO, SLA, and error budget.

Interview Answer

An SLI is a quantitative measure of service behavior, such as availability, latency, or error rate. An SLO is the target for an SLI over a defined measurement window. An SLA is an explicit or implicit agreement with users that includes consequences when agreed service objectives are not met. An error budget is the amount of SLO non-compliance the service can tolerate within that window. For a ratio-based 99.9% availability SLO, for example, the corresponding error budget is 0.1%.

شرح مبسط

الـSLI هو quantitative measurement لسلوك الخدمة، مثل availability أو latency أو error rate. الـSLO هو target لهذا الـSLI خلال measurement window محددة. الـSLA هو اتفاق مع المستخدمين وله consequences عند عدم تحقيق مستوى الخدمة المتفق عليه. أما Error Budget فهو مقدار عدم تحقيق الـSLO الذي يمكن تحمله خلال الفترة. مثلًا في availability SLO بنسبة 99.9% يكون الـerror budget المقابل 0.1%.

Q58Architecture

What would you do if Product wants faster releases but production reliability is deteriorating?

Interview Answer

I would make the trade-off data-driven rather than turn it into an Architecture-versus-Product argument. I would review user-facing SLO performance, incident trends, change failures, recovery time, and business impact. Ideally, Product and Engineering would already have an agreed error-budget policy defining what actions to take as the budget approaches exhaustion or is exhausted. Depending on that policy, we might reduce risky releases and shift capacity toward root causes, testing, or resilience until reliability recovers. The important point is to agree on the reliability objective and response policy before a crisis occurs.

شرح مبسط

أحول القرار إلى data-driven trade-off بدل صراع بين Architecture وProduct. أراجع user-facing SLO performance والincident trends والchange failures والrecovery time والbusiness impact. والأفضل أن يكون Product وEngineering متفقين مسبقًا على Error-Budget Policy تحدد الإجراءات عندما يقترب الـbudget من النفاد أو ينفد. وحسب هذه السياسة قد نقلل risky releases ونحول جزءًا من capacity إلى root causes أو testing أو resilience حتى تتحسن reliability.

Q59Leadership

How do you lead architecture across multiple engineering teams without becoming a bottleneck?

Interview Answer

I would centralize principles, reference architectures, security and compliance guardrails, and the highest-risk cross-platform decisions, but decentralize routine implementation decisions to capable teams. I would establish architecture owners or senior technical leaders within domains, use regular design reviews for significant decisions, publish reusable patterns, and maintain a clear escalation path. My goal is architectural consistency without requiring the Director to approve every technical decision.

شرح مبسط

أجعل principles وreference architectures وsecurity/compliance guardrails والقرارات عالية المخاطر مركزية، لكن routine implementation decisions تكون decentralized للفرق القادرة. أحدد architecture owners أو senior technical leaders لكل domain، وأستخدم design reviews للقرارات المهمة وأنشر reusable patterns وأحدد escalation path واضحة. الهدف architectural consistency بدون أن يحتاج كل قرار لموافقة الـDirector.

Q60Leadership

What would your first 90 days look like as Director of Solutions Architecture at Santechture?

Interview Answer

In the first month, I would learn before changing: understand the product and integration architecture, engineering operating model, critical customer workflows, GCC regulatory commitments, incident history, delivery constraints, and technical-debt profile. In the second month, I would baseline the major architecture risks and role KPIs, assess the existing architecture strategy and roadmap, and either validate or refine them with the CTO, Product, PMO, Security, Data, and Engineering leaders.
