# Research Basis

The product architecture was informed by:

- LinkedIn Talent Solutions (2025): skills-first hiring and quality-of-hire evidence. Companies with the most skills-based searches were reported as more likely to make quality hires; recruiters increasingly emphasize accurate skill assessment.
- OECD Digital Government Policy Framework: six dimensions — digital by design, data-driven public sector, government as a platform, open by default, user-driven and proactiveness.
- OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026: emphasis on turning digital maturity into institutional performance and delivery capacity.
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 and Generative AI Profile: voluntary risk-management concepts for trustworthy/responsible AI.
- Existing Mahmoud Salama evidence bank and recruiter/case-study pages in the Website repository.

The assessment modules are decision-support tools and do not claim certification, formal conformity, financial audit, or official OECD/NIST scoring.
