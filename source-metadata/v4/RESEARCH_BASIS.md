# Wave 4 Research Basis

Wave 4 focuses on four gaps left after the first three waves: **career advancement**, **executive-search / board visibility**, **external authority**, and **brand-quality protection**.

## Research synthesis

- LinkedIn/Edelman research supports moving from self-promotional material toward research-backed, useful, human thought leadership and distinctive presentation.
- Google Search Central emphasizes people-first content, first-hand expertise, accurate authorship and author/entity linkage.
- 2026 Middle East technology research from PwC shows that AI, agentic AI, cloud sovereignty, data readiness and cyber trust are now executive operating issues rather than isolated technology experiments.
- Spencer Stuart and Egon Zehnder guidance supports distinct board bios, board-specific value propositions, profile raising and relationship-based board search rather than treating board roles like ordinary advertised jobs.
- GLG and Guidepoint provide compliant routes for experienced professionals to participate in paid expert engagements.
- ORCID provides a durable research identity that can link publications, datasets and affiliations over time.
