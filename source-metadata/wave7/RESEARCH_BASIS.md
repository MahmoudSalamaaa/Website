# Wave 7 Research Basis

Wave 7 is designed around five current career-market principles:

1. **Skills-first hiring:** demonstrate capabilities through evidence and achievements, not title matching alone.
2. **AI-assisted sourcing:** make role intent, skills, evidence and career facts semantically clear and machine-readable.
3. **Thought-leadership credibility:** use research, data, practical guidance and firsthand case studies to earn executive attention.
4. **People-first discoverability:** publish useful, attributable, experience-based material instead of search-engine filler.
5. **Executive-search readiness:** build clear positioning, visibility and search relationships before the moment of urgent need.

Primary research families reviewed: LinkedIn Talent Solutions Future of Recruiting / skills-first hiring / AI-assisted search; LinkedIn-Edelman B2B Thought Leadership research; Google Search Central people-first content guidance; Spencer Stuart executive job-search guidance.

## Design conclusion
The existing main homepage should not be redesigned. Wave 7 adds a conversion and semantic layer around the existing page. The default homepage remains visually and textually unchanged. Role-specific copy is activated only by an explicit `?view=` campaign parameter.
