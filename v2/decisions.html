Decisions

The invisible layer of every system.

Technology leadership is a sequence of trade-offs. These are the kinds of decisions I care about.

01

Replace vs. Refactor

When structural constraints block the target state, modernization becomes a transformation problem rather than a code-cleanup exercise.

02

Build vs. Buy

Evaluate strategic differentiation, institutional capability, TCO, vendor dependency, time, integration and long-term operability — not licensing price alone.

03

Centralize vs. Distribute

Centralize what needs consistency and governance; distribute what needs domain ownership and speed. The boundary is the architecture decision.

04

Resilience vs. Complexity

Availability has a cost. Design recovery and failure handling around business criticality rather than adding infrastructure for its own sake.

05

Control vs. Usability

Security and governance should become part of the workflow. A control that users constantly bypass is an architectural failure signal.

Good architecture is often the art of saying “not this.”
Not every integration needs to be real-time.
Not every platform needs to become microservices.
Not every control belongs in a policy document.
Not every modernization problem is a cloud problem.
