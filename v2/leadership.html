Leadership operating model

My leadership model connects strategy, architecture, delivery and operations rather than treating them as separate layers. Decision rights are explicit; technical depth remains close enough to challenge assumptions and unblock delivery.

01 · Direction

Translate strategy into architecture

Turn institutional priorities and constraints into roadmaps, target states, investment choices and coherent platform boundaries.

02 · Governance

Make decision rights visible

Portfolio priorities, architecture decisions, security, data, vendors, releases and production readiness need named ownership and escalation paths.

03 · Delivery

Close the loop to operations

Requirements, engineering, QA/UAT, deployment, cutover, training, support and continuous improvement belong to one lifecycle.

04 · People

Grow capability, not dependency

Build multidisciplinary internal depth and create space for teams to own implementation while leadership owns the system-level decisions.

05 · Commercial

Use build-vs-buy deliberately

Compare capability fit, TCO, time-to-market, control, resilience, knowledge retention and integration before choosing the operating model.

06 · Reliability

Treat operations as design input

Capacity, continuity, incident/RCA, monitoring, backup/recovery and service readiness inform architecture before go-live.
