# V4 WOW Review — What changed and why

## Overall direction
The previous wave fixed logic, wording, hierarchy and evidence. This WOW wave keeps that content baseline and adds executive-level visual distinction without turning the site into a gimmick.

## index.html
- Added a compact proof line directly under the hero copy: 54-person organization, EGP 150M budget, Egypt & Oman, national platforms.
- Added richer hero gradients, stronger metric treatment, subtle card motion and a cleaner visual hierarchy.
Why: the first screen now proves seniority before the visitor scrolls.

## portfolio.html
- Kept the reviewed narrative intact and applied the shared WOW visual layer: stronger section separation, depth, hover response and richer visual rhythm.
Why: Portfolio already had the right content; it needed premium presentation rather than more text.

## projects.html
- Preserved the five categories.
- Made the first four projects visually stronger as flagship entries.
- Added subtle image zoom and category-colored top borders.
- Replaced repeated “Internal / historical project” with the lighter “Private / internal”.
Why: the projects themselves should dominate the page, not filters or evidence labels.

## technologies.html
- Added a four-part capability rail: ARCHITECT / LEAD / DELIVER / OPERATE.
- Kept all capability data and the simplified filters.
Why: a recruiter now understands the executive technology profile before seeing 161 detailed capabilities.

## architecture.html
- Elevated the flagship architecture case visually.
- Added stronger spatial hierarchy around the architecture method and SAP integration case.
Why: the page should feel like architecture thinking, not a text list of architecture terms.

## experience.html
- Added a visual progression ribbon: Systems → Software → Team Leadership → Enterprise Delivery → Technology Executive.
Why: the career story becomes instantly understandable before the detailed timeline.

## leadership.html
- Made the current 54-person leadership mandate the dominant evidence card.
Why: current scope is the strongest leadership proof and should visually outrank historical evidence.

## governance.html
- Added a stronger governance presentation with a colored institutional-accountability spine.
Why: governance should feel like a board/executive responsibility, not three ordinary cards.

## awards.html
- Enhanced the recognition cards with a visual evidence spine while keeping contribution wording precise.
Why: recognition now feels important without overstating personal ownership of team awards.

## about.html
- Made “Professional identity” the dominant card.
Why: the page should lead with who Mahmoud is professionally, not treat identity, geography and education as equal-weight facts.

## contact.html
- Made Email the primary full-width conversion card.
Why: Contact should end the site with one obvious next action instead of equal-weight destinations.

## Shared WOW layer
- Translucent executive navigation.
- Controlled cyan/violet/coral gradients.
- Stronger card elevation and hover response.
- Section separators and improved rhythm.
- Image motion on project cards.
- Reduced-motion accessibility preserved.
- No dark redesign, neon UI, 3D gimmicks, or childish animation.
