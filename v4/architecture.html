ARCHITECTURE DECISION LOG

Selected decisions and why they matter.

INTEGRATION

Bi-directional enterprise integration

Connect request and financial workflows across UPA and SAP environments.

Why: preserve end-to-end operational continuity and reconciliation.

DATA

Master-data harmonization before scale

Align items, suppliers, codes, units and classifications before production exchange.

Why: reduce mapping ambiguity and downstream reconciliation risk.

OPERATIONS

Production readiness as architecture

Include QA, UAT, logging, monitoring, support and post-go-live ownership.

Why: architecture must survive real operations, not only design review.
