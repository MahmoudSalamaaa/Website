1. Cost is architecture, not accounting afterthought

التكلفة تحتاج owner وaccount/tag وbudget وreview؛ لا يكفي أن تعرف سعر EC2.

2. Purchasing models

ModelBest fitMain riskManager sentence
On-DemandUnknown, short-lived or variable demand.Higher unit cost if used for a stable baseline forever.Buy flexibility until the usage pattern is proven.
Savings PlansStable compute usage where commitment is justified.Over-commitment.Commit only the stable base after measuring.
Reserved InstancesSpecific steady service usage and reservation benefits.Wrong term/configuration or unused reservation.Understand service-specific flexibility before purchase.
SpotInterruptible, fault-tolerant batch/workers.Capacity can be reclaimed.Never make irreplaceable production depend on one Spot instance.
Dedicated Host/InstanceLicensing, isolation or compliance-driven cases.Higher cost / complexity.Use only when a real requirement justifies it.
Capacity ReservationNeed EC2 capacity available in a specific AZ.Paying for reserved capacity without matching usage.Capacity guarantee and price discount are separate decisions.

3. Cost tools - know the job of each

AWS Budgets

Thresholds, forecasts and alerts/actions for cost or usage.

إنذار مبكر وbudget guardrail.

Cost Explorer

Interactive analysis of cost/usage trends, drivers and forecasts.

تحليل السبب والاتجاه.

Cost & Usage Reports

Detailed cost and usage data for deeper reporting/allocation.

أدق بيانات billing/usage.

Pricing Calculator

Estimate planned workload or bill changes before commitment.

Estimate قبل القرار.

Organizations

Consolidated account governance/billing; use accounts and tags for ownership.

Multi-account + consolidated billing.

Trusted Advisor

Automated recommendations/checks; combine with Well-Architected workload reviews.

Checks وليست governance كاملة.

4. Critical 2026 correction - AWS Support plans

Your handwritten table is historical. Do not walk into the interview quoting the old Developer / Business / Enterprise On-Ramp structure as the current plan catalogue.
2026 current directionWhat to rememberInterview use
BasicAccount/billing assistance plus self-service resources and health/service tools.Not a production technical-response plan.
Business Support+Current enhanced business support with 24x7 expert access and fast critical response.Production operations needing expert support.
Enterprise SupportMission-critical support with designated strategic/technical guidance and fastest enterprise response.Critical enterprise estate and complex operations.
Unified OperationsHighest integrated operations/support model in the current documentation.Know the name and purpose; exact commercial fit is an organizational decision.

AWS has announced end-of-support transitions for Developer Support, Business Support and Enterprise On-Ramp effective January 1, 2027; existing customers may still be on them during 2026.

5. WaCA FinOps answer

1 · Allocate

Account + project/country/owner tags.

2 · Alert

Budgets and anomaly signals before month-end surprise.

3 · Optimize

Rightsize, schedule, storage lifecycle, commitments.

4 · Protect

Do not trade away backup, logging or security to save money.

6. Quick retrieval quiz

Which tool tells you that spend is about to cross a threshold?
AWS Budgets.
Which tool is best to explore why EC2 cost increased over the last month?
Cost Explorer.
Which dataset gives the deepest line-item cost/usage detail?
Cost and Usage Reports / current detailed data export.
A workload can be interrupted and restarted. Which purchasing model may offer the deepest discount?
Spot. Only if the workload tolerates interruption.
