Review last; do not sacrifice P0 delivery quality for these.

Evidence boundary: OCB feedback documents are excellent evidence of MSF management culture but are not proof that WaCA uses the exact same HR procedure. MSF Egypt Internal Regulations are Egypt-specific conduct rules; use the principles, not Egypt legal timelines, when speaking about WaCA.
Interview trap: Do not answer like a generic cloud architect. Every technical answer should connect to operational continuity, field constraints, sensitive data, people, governance and clear ownership.
