What the vacancy is really hiring for

An enterprise systems owner working under the Head of Information Systems, with responsibility across headquarters and missions.

Primary objective

Lead management and optimisation of enterprise IT systems, ensuring security, availability and performance while aligning local systems with shared MSF services.

Operating interfaces

Head of IS, Security, Support, shared technology services, functional owners, vendors, project teams and mission stakeholders.
