VERIFIED STUDY GUIDE

MSF Applications — What You Need to Know

Built from public MSF sources. The key rule is simple: distinguish what MSF publicly documents from what is specifically proven for WaCA. Never invent the current production architecture.

1 · UniField

MAIN MSF APPLICATION

Safe definition: UniField is one of the main MSF applications. Public MSF material places it in finance and supply contexts, and MSF's official Applications course trains ICT staff to install, update, maintain and troubleshoot it.

ICT responsibilities documented by MSF: machine preparation and installation; backup configuration and monitoring; troubleshooting; machine maintenance; OS updates; UniField patching.

Supply relationship: UniCat explicitly names UniField as an example of a downstream field/order tool receiving subscribed article information.

Product/master data → operational ordering/supply context → UniField → field operations

Interview angle: availability, backup/restore, patching, synchronization, support, data quality, lifecycle and field continuity.

Evidence: MSF Tembo “MSF Applications”; MSF UniCat FAQ/current product pages.

2 · eHealth

MAIN MSF APPLICATION

Safe definition: eHealth is explicitly included in MSF's Applications training for ICT Officers.

What ICT is trained on: its ICT role, troubleshooting involving Nestor, firewall and the application, plus escalation procedure and communication.

Interview angle: you do not need to pretend clinical-product expertise. Show that you understand the IS Manager's responsibility for the technical environment, secure connectivity, troubleshooting ownership and escalation.

Evidence: MSF Tembo “MSF Applications”.

3 · UniData

CENTRAL MASTER DATA

Safe definition: UniData is the central MSF article database. MSF says many other systems link to it for product information.

It was introduced to centralize product creation, avoid duplication, improve descriptions and share article changes across the MSF movement.

UniData = source/master data
→ trusted article/product information
→ downstream MSF systems

Interview angle: master-data ownership, quality, governance, controlled change, integration and reconciliation.

Evidence: MSF glossary; official UniCat/SPINCO introduction.

4 · UniCat

PRODUCT CATALOGUE

Safe definition: UniCat is MSF's web catalogue for humanitarian product information and is updated daily from UniData.

Important concept: subscriptions determine whether article information flows to an Operational Centre's downstream order tool or database. WaCA appears among current OC subscription options on UniCat.

UniData → daily update → UniCat → OC subscription → downstream tools

Interview angle: understand the distinction: UniData is the master-data system; UniCat is the catalogue/visibility layer.

Evidence: official UniCat FAQ, introduction and current product pages.

5 · SITS

NOT AN APPLICATION

Safe definition: SITS means Shared IT Services. MSF established it to streamline shared technology services, reduce duplication and improve service quality.

Public MSF documentation lists service areas including Azure, Microsoft 365, Security, Infrastructure and Licenses. SITS can contribute from design and build through implementation and change/communication management.

Interview angle: start with MSF/global standards, work with shared services, document local requirements and tightly control exceptions.

Evidence: MSF Intersectional Evaluation Group SITS assessment.

6 · Microsoft stack

DIRECT ROLE REQUIREMENT

The WaCA IS Manager vacancy explicitly calls for experience around Windows Server, Microsoft 365/O365, Azure and MDM.

Interview angle: identity-first security, MFA, least privilege, privileged administration, endpoint compliance, licensing, monitoring, lifecycle, backup/recovery and supportability.

Accuracy rule: do not claim knowledge of WaCA's exact tenant topology, MDM configuration or Azure workloads unless the panel confirms them.

7 · NetSuite

WACA CONTEXT

Safe statement: public WaCA recruitment material provides evidence of Oracle NetSuite implementation, integration and optimization work in its Information Systems environment.

Interview angle: migration, data quality, access roles, interfaces, UAT, reconciliation, cutover/rollback, support, documentation and adoption.

Do not say as fact: “NetSuite is HQ and UniField is field” unless the panel confirms that this is the current production architecture.

8 · What remains unknown

VERIFY WITH PANEL

Public evidence does not give us WaCA's complete current application inventory, exact integration diagram, hosting topology, final NetSuite/UniField coexistence model, system-by-system ownership matrix or every production interface.

Strong interview behavior: state your researched understanding, then explicitly say you would validate the current architecture, criticality, ownership and roadmap.

Best answer: “What do you know about our systems landscape?”

“From my research, I understand that MSF has a mixed systems landscape combining field applications, central master data and shared technology services. UniField is one of the main MSF applications, with defined processes for installation, backup, maintenance, patching and troubleshooting. UniData is the central product master-data source, while UniCat provides catalogue visibility and subscriptions can feed downstream order or field tools. MSF also trains ICT teams to support the technical environment around eHealth. On the shared-services side, SITS provides capabilities around Microsoft 365, Azure, security, infrastructure and licensing. For WaCA specifically, I have also seen public evidence of NetSuite transformation work. I would not assume the exact current production architecture from public information. My first step would be to confirm system ownership, integrations, criticality, support boundaries and the current roadmap with the team.”

Designed as a 60–75 second panel answer.

Memory shortcut

UniData = master product data → UniCat = catalogue/visibility/subscriptions → UniField = major operational field application with finance/supply context. eHealth = health application environment with ICT troubleshooting/escalation. SITS = shared IT services, not an app. M365/Azure/MDM/Windows = direct role stack. NetSuite = WaCA transformation context; confirm exact architecture.
