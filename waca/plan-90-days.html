30–60–90 day plan

Discover → stabilise → govern and improve.

Days 1–30

Discover & align

  • Meet IS, Support, Security, shared-service contacts and business owners
  • Map systems, integrations, AWS/Azure environments, identity, licences, contracts and costs
  • Review incidents, risks, audits and project commitments
  • Identify critical services and RTO/RPO

Outputs

Stakeholder map, application register, top-risk list, listening report.

Days 31–60

Stabilise & prioritise

  • Validate cloud controls, privileged access, patching and MDM coverage
  • Review backup/recovery evidence and schedule restore tests
  • Clarify RACI across teams and vendors
  • Identify EOL systems and undocumented dependencies

Outputs

Risk treatment plan, service RACI, quick wins, draft roadmap.

Days 61–90

Deliver & institutionalise

  • Deliver two or three visible improvements
  • Approve portfolio and documentation standards
  • Launch systems scorecard and service review
  • Present 12–18 month roadmap and capacity plan

Outputs

Measured quick wins, approved roadmap, KPI baseline, governance calendar.

Interview phrasing: “I would not arrive with a pre-selected technology programme. I would first establish evidence, relationships and operational priorities, then deliver quick wins that support a credible roadmap.”
