30–60–90 day plan

A credible first-quarter plan for joining an existing systems function during an active ERP transition. The theme is discover → stabilise → govern → improve.

Days 1–30

Discover & align

  • Meet Head of IS, Security, Support, SITS/shared-service contacts and functional owners
  • Map enterprise applications, owners, criticality, integrations, environments, licences, vendors and support contracts
  • Confirm the current NetSuite / UniField / SIRA transition phase and project decision rights
  • Review incidents, known risks, audits, changes, migrations and go-live commitments
  • Identify critical services, data owners, RTO/RPO and current recovery evidence
  • Interview representative missions to understand connectivity, support and adoption constraints

Outputs

Application/service register, stakeholder & RACI map, integration map, top-risk list and listening report.

Days 31–60

Stabilise & prioritise

  • Validate privileged access, M365/Azure/endpoint controls and system admin practices
  • Review backup/restore evidence and schedule tests for highest-criticality services
  • Assess NetSuite/UniField migration, interface reconciliation, UAT/cutover and support readiness
  • Identify EOL systems, undocumented dependencies and single-person knowledge
  • Reconcile licences, vendor SLAs, escalation paths and security obligations
  • Create a prioritised backlog using operational value, risk, cost, dependencies and field impact

Outputs

Risk-treatment plan, recovery-test calendar, ERP operational-readiness gaps, service RACI and draft roadmap.

Days 61–90

Deliver & institutionalise

  • Deliver two or three evidence-backed quick wins without destabilising ERP delivery
  • Approve lifecycle, change/release, documentation and operational-acceptance standards
  • Launch a systems scorecard and monthly service review
  • Establish integration ownership, control totals/reconciliation and data-quality accountability
  • Establish vendor/licence review and access-review calendars
  • Present a 12–18 month roadmap including transition, retirement, resilience and capacity needs

Outputs

Measured quick wins, approved roadmap, KPI baseline, governance calendar and transition-support model.

Possible quick wins — only after validation

Recovery evidence

Prove one critical restore end-to-end and close the observed gap.

Admin hardening

Review privileged identities, emergency access and MFA/Conditional Access coverage.

Integration register

Document owners, data contract, retry/reconciliation and support path for critical interfaces.

Licence / support cleanup

Recover unused licences and make vendor escalation and renewal ownership explicit.

What I would not do in the first 90 days

No premature redesign: I would not arrive promising a new ERP, cloud migration or organizational restructure before understanding the transition already in flight. The first-quarter objective is to make current commitments safer, clarify ownership, prove recovery and build an evidence-based roadmap.
Interview phrasing: “I would not arrive with a pre-selected technology programme. My first commitment is to understand the portfolio and the ERP transition already under way, establish evidence and decision rights, close immediate continuity/security gaps, and then agree a roadmap with the Head of IS and functional owners.”
