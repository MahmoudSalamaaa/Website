Evidence hierarchy

Use this wording discipline in the interview.

1 · WaCA confirmedUse direct language only where public WaCA evidence supports it.
2 · MSF movement evidenceSay “MSF uses…” but confirm exact WaCA deployment.
3 · OCB evidenceUse as management/process evidence; do not label it WaCA policy.
4 · MSF Egypt policyTransfer principles, not Egypt-specific legal timelines or local HR rules.
5 · AWS notebookKeep the concept, then verify current AWS service name/lifecycle before answering.
