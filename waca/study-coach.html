Two answer levels for every question

Quick answer

30–45 seconds

Memorize the structure and keywords, not every word. Use it for a direct first response and expand only when the panel asks for detail.

Full professional answer

60–120 seconds

Use this to understand the complete logic, controls, trade-offs and MSF relevance. It is your depth layer, not a script to recite word-for-word.

How to rate a question

Again

Could not answer

Return today / tomorrow. Repair the concept, not the wording.

Hard

Partial / shaky

Review tomorrow and again after a few days.

Good

Correct with effort

Space it several days; then answer in a different scenario.

Easy

Automatic + transferable

Push it out; spend time on weaker material.

Research-backed changes applied to the project

Retrieval before reveal

The question bank and mocks force an answer attempt before model answers. Based on the testing-effect literature.

Spaced adaptive review

Ratings create due dates so the same question returns after increasing intervals.

Interleaved mocks

Question types are deliberately mixed to make you identify the right framework/service under uncertainty.

Self-explanation prompts

AWS cards emphasize “why this service” and “why not the alternative” to improve transfer rather than rote recall.

Learning-design references

Retrieval: Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Test-enhanced learning. Spacing: Cepeda et al. (2006), distributed practice meta-analysis. Interleaving: Rohrer & Taylor (2007), shuffled mathematics practice. Self-explanation: Chi et al. (1989).

These studies guide the interaction design; they do not dictate a single perfect schedule. The 20-day intervals are deliberately simple and interview-oriented.
