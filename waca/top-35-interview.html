How would you assess an enterprise application portfolio you have just inherited?

Interview-ready answer

I would build a single inventory with business owner, technical owner, users, criticality, data sensitivity, integrations, hosting, vendor, license, support status, recovery requirement and lifecycle stage. Then I would combine incidents, user feedback, cost, security findings, technical debt and business dependency to create a risk-and-value view. I would validate it with functional owners and field representatives because an application that looks low-value from HQ may be operationally critical in a mission.

10

How would you decide which applications to retain, upgrade, replace or retire?

Interview-ready answer

I would use agreed criteria: business criticality, user value, security risk, reliability, integration fit, vendor viability, maintainability, total cost, data portability and strategic alignment. Retain when value and risk remain acceptable, upgrade when the platform is sound but aging, replace when there is a clear business case, and retire only after dependencies and data-retention needs are addressed. I would never replace a system simply because a newer technology exists.

12

How would you manage a transition from a legacy ERP or application to a new platform?

Interview-ready answer

I would start with process and data, not configuration. First define scope, business owners, target processes, controls and success criteria. Then map data, integrations, roles, reporting and exceptions. I would use phased testing: configuration validation, integration testing, migration rehearsals and business UAT. Before cutover I need go/no-go criteria, rollback, communications, training and support. After go-live I would run hypercare with daily issue triage and reconciliation.

الشرح بالعربي

رتّب الإجابة: process → data → integration → roles → testing → cutover → hypercare.

13

How would you migrate master and transactional data safely?

Interview-ready answer

I would define authoritative sources and data owners first. Then profile quality, resolve duplicates, document transformations and map source fields to the target model. Migration scripts should be repeatable with validation totals and exception reports. I prefer at least one full rehearsal before production cutover. After migration, business owners should sign off reconciliation, not only IT. Sensitive data also requires controlled access, secure transfer and retention rules.

الشرح بالعربي

المهم auditability: owner، cleansing، mapping، rehearsal، validation، reconciliation، sign-off.

14

How would you validate reconciliation after migration?

Interview-ready answer

I would define reconciliation rules before migration. Depending on the system, that can include record counts, control totals, financial balances, open transactions, inventory quantities, key reference data and sampled end-to-end business cases. Automated comparisons should highlight exceptions, each exception needs an owner, and functional owners should sign off because technical row counts do not prove business correctness.

الشرح بالعربي

row count وحده لا يكفي. استخدم balances، business totals، exceptions وbusiness sign-off.

15

How would you manage roles and segregation of duties in an ERP?

Interview-ready answer

I would start from business responsibilities, not individual requests. Roles should follow least privilege and separate incompatible activities such as requesting, approving, paying or administering the system. I would use role templates, documented approvals for exceptions, periodic privileged-access reviews and strong joiner-mover-leaver controls. Any operational exception should be time-bound, approved and supported by compensating controls.

الشرح بالعربي

SoD يمنع شخصًا واحدًا من التحكم في دورة حساسة كاملة. اربطه بـleast privilege وJML وreviews.

16

How would you plan UAT, cutover, rollback and hypercare?

Interview-ready answer

UAT should be business-led and cover critical workflows, edge cases, permissions, integrations and reporting using agreed acceptance criteria. Cutover needs a detailed runbook with owners, timing, communication and go/no-go checkpoints. Rollback triggers must be defined in advance. Hypercare then provides enhanced support, daily issue review, severity management and reconciliation until the service can transition safely to normal operations.

الشرح بالعربي

UAT business-led، cutover runbook، rollback criteria، ثم hypercare وانتقال منظم للتشغيل.

Recovery is not complete until transactions are reconciled and the root cause is addressed.

18

How would you support field users with unreliable or low-bandwidth connectivity?

Interview-ready answer

I would identify workflows that must continue offline or with intermittent access, minimize payloads and unnecessary synchronization, use retry and queue mechanisms where supported, and provide clear local procedures. The objective is graceful degradation: users should know what still works, what must wait and how data will reconcile later.

الشرح بالعربي

Standard users receive role-based access, while privileged accounts are separate, strongly authenticated and tightly monitored. I would review dormant accounts, external identities, group ownership and emergency access regularly.

22

Walk me through a Joiner–Mover–Leaver process.

Interview-ready answer

For a joiner, the approved role should drive identity, groups, application access, device setup and training. For a mover, access must be recalculated rather than only adding new permissions; old rights should be removed. For a leaver, disable access at the agreed time, revoke sessions and privileged credentials, recover devices, transfer approved data ownership and preserve required records. Periodic access reviews are the safety net for anything missed.

الشرح بالعربي

أهم نقطة في Mover: لا تضف صلاحيات جديدة وتترك القديمة. وفي Leaver: identity + sessions + devices + data ownership.

23

What would you do if a privileged or admin account were compromised?

Interview-ready answer

I would treat it as a high-severity security incident. First contain the account: block or restrict access, revoke active sessions and rotate affected credentials while preserving evidence. Then determine scope using sign-in, audit and change logs. I would coordinate with security, service owners and business leadership because containment can affect operations. Recovery includes restoring safe access, validating critical configurations and monitoring for recurrence, followed by stronger privileged-access controls.

الشرح بالعربي

الترتيب: contain → preserve evidence → scope → recover → improve. لا تحذف الأدلة أثناء الاستجابة.

24

How do you control vendor or third-party privileged access?

Interview-ready answer

Vendor access should be based on a defined support need, not convenience. I would require an accountable internal owner, least privilege, strong authentication, time-bounded access where possible, logging and periodic review. Shared accounts should be avoided. Contracts should address data handling, incident notification and offboarding. If a vendor asks for permanent Global Admin or equivalent rights, my default is to redesign the support method rather than accept unnecessary standing privilege.

الشرح بالعربي

Vendor access ليس دائمًا: internal owner، least privilege، MFA، time-bound، logging، review، revocation.

25

Explain RTO and RPO and how you would determine them for critical systems.

Interview-ready answer

RTO is the maximum acceptable time to restore a service after disruption. RPO is the maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time. I would not set either from technology preference. I would work with business owners to understand operational impact, then design backup, replication and recovery procedures to meet the agreed targets. Higher recovery targets cost more, so the decision must balance mission impact, risk and resources.

الشرح بالعربي

RTO = وقت استعادة الخدمة. RPO = مقدار البيانات المقبول فقدها زمنيًا. يحددهما business impact وليس IT وحده.

26

How do you know a backup strategy actually works?

Interview-ready answer

A successful backup job only proves that the backup process reported success. I need evidence that data can be restored within the required time and is usable. That means scheduled restore tests, integrity checks, documented procedures and recovery exercises for critical systems. I would also verify retention, isolation or immutability where appropriate, and record actual recovery times and gaps. If we have never restored from a backup, I would not claim that disaster recovery is tested.

الشرح بالعربي

backup success لا يساوي recovery success. الاختبار الحقيقي هو restore فعلي مع قياس الزمن وصحة البيانات.

How would you design and test disaster recovery for critical systems?

Interview-ready answer

I would begin with critical services and agreed RTO/RPO, then map dependencies such as identity, DNS, networks, databases, integrations, keys, external vendors and support contacts. The recovery design should match those dependencies. I want a documented runbook with decision authority, technical steps, communications and business validation. Testing should include component restores and scenario exercises with actual timings and corrective actions.

الشرح بالعربي

لا تنسَ dependencies. DR خطة تشغيلية واختبار دوري، وليس ملفًا مكتوبًا فقط.

28

Walk us through your response to ransomware or destructive activity.

Interview-ready answer

I would activate incident response and focus first on safety, scope and containment. Isolate affected identities or systems without unnecessarily destroying evidence, protect unaffected backups and determine whether activity is still active. Security teams analyze logs while business leaders understand continuity options. Recovery should use known-clean systems and validated backups, with compromised credentials and entry points addressed before restoring trust. After recovery, document root cause, lessons and control improvements.

الشرح بالعربي

containment أولًا، حماية backups، معرفة scope، clean recovery، ثم root cause والتحسين.

Interview-ready answer

Roles should be explicit: incident lead, security investigation, service owner, mission representative and communications. Updates should be factual and scheduled. Recovery includes data-integrity and business reconciliation checks, followed by corrective actions with owners and deadlines.

30

How would you protect confidential medical or employee information while troubleshooting a system?

Interview-ready answer

I would apply need-to-know access and minimize the data used for troubleshooting. Where possible, reproduce the issue with test or masked data rather than copying sensitive production records. If production access is necessary, it should be authorized, limited, logged and removed when the task ends. I would avoid uncontrolled screenshots or exports and share operational status without exposing medical or personal details that are not needed.

الشرح بالعربي

المفتاح need-to-know وdata minimization. استخدم masked/test data وتجنب نسخ بيانات حساسة دون ضرورة.

31

Explain AWS shared responsibility and how it changes across EC2, RDS and Lambda.

Interview-ready answer

AWS secures the cloud infrastructure while the customer secures what they configure and operate in the cloud. With EC2, the customer manages more: guest OS, applications, identities, security groups and data. With RDS, AWS manages more of the underlying platform, but the customer still owns identities, network exposure, configuration and data. With Lambda, AWS manages servers and runtime infrastructure while the customer focuses on code, dependencies, permissions, secrets, event sources and data. Managed services reduce some operational responsibility; they do not remove security responsibility.

الشرح بالعربي

كلما استخدمت managed service يقل ما تديره من infrastructure، لكن يبقى عليك IAM، data، configuration وapplication security.

32

What are IAM users, IAM roles and IAM Identity Center, and when would you use each?

Interview-ready answer

An IAM user is a long-lived identity inside an AWS account and should be used sparingly for human access. IAM roles provide temporary credentials and are the normal choice for workloads, cross-account access and delegated administration. IAM Identity Center centralizes workforce access across AWS accounts and applications. My preference is centralized identity, MFA, short-lived credentials, least privilege and role-based access rather than permanent access keys for people.

الشرح بالعربي

Users أقل تفضيلًا للبشر، roles لصلاحيات مؤقتة وworkloads، وIdentity Center لإدارة وصول الموظفين مركزيًا.

33

A security alert appears: how would you use GuardDuty, CloudTrail and Config to investigate it?

Interview-ready answer

I would use GuardDuty as a detection signal, not the full investigation. I would validate the finding, identify affected resources or identities and contain urgent risk. CloudTrail helps answer who or what called AWS APIs, from where and when. AWS Config helps show resource configuration state and changes over time. I would combine those sources with service logs and business context, preserve evidence, remediate the cause and confirm the environment is back to an approved state.

الشرح بالعربي

GuardDuty ينبه، CloudTrail يوضح API activity، وConfig يوضح configuration state/history. لا تخلط بين أدوارهم.

34

Tell us about an underperforming team member. How did you handle it?

Interview-ready answer

I start by separating the person from the performance issue. I clarify the expected result, use specific examples, listen for causes such as unclear priorities, capability gaps or workload, and agree measurable improvement actions. I provide support through coaching, training or closer check-ins and document progress fairly. If performance does not improve despite clarity and support, I involve HR and follow the formal process rather than improvising discipline. My goal is development where possible, but also accountability and fairness to the team.

الشرح بالعربي

ابدأ بالتوضيح والدعم والـfeedback، ثم صعّد رسميًا فقط عند الحاجة. لا تبدأ بالعقوبة.

35

How would you handle a confidentiality, safeguarding, conflict-of-interest or vendor-integrity concern involving someone on your team?

Interview-ready answer

I would take the concern seriously, protect confidentiality and use the correct reporting or safeguarding channel. I would ensure there is no retaliation against someone raising a concern in good faith. I would preserve relevant information but would not conduct an unauthorized personal investigation or confront people in a way that could compromise a formal process. For conflicts of interest or vendor integrity, I would disclose the issue, remove conflicted decision-makers where required and keep decisions transparent and documented. The manager’s role is to protect people and process, not become judge and investigator.

الشرح بالعربي

لا تحقق بنفسك في safeguarding complaint. حافظ على السرية، استخدم القناة الرسمية، امنع retaliation، وفي COI/vendor integrity استخدم disclosure وrecusal والشفافية.
